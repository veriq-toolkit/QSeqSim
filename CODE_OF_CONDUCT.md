# Code of Conduct

QSeqSim adopts the [Qiskit community Code of
Conduct](https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/docs/en/guides/code-of-conduct). Its
expectations of respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free participation apply
to this repository, its issue tracker, pull requests, and project communication.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer address listed in
`pyproject.toml`. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter
privacy as far as practical, and may remove content, restrict participation, or
take other proportionate action. Maintainers must recuse themselves when they
are involved in a report.
